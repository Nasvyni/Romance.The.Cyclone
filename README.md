# Romance.The.Cyclone
A dating-sim style game built in Twine based on the cult hit musical Ride the Cyclone. Explore a strange town, visit the traveling carnival (Wonder Ville), date a dope cast of characters (Constance, Noel, Mischa, Ricky, Jane, and Ocean)!

Welcome to the repo for my game built in **Twine**! This project combines a slice-of-life school and town simulator with the eerie, mysterious atmosphere of a traveling carnival of Uranium City!

Based on Ride the Cyclone is a 2009 musical with music, lyrics and book by Jacob Richmond and Brooke Maxwell.[1] It is the second installment in Richmond's "Uranium Teen Scream Trilogy", a collection of three theatrical works, one not yet written, that take place in the exaggerated Uranium City.

The story revolves around members of the St. Cassian High School chamber choir of Uranium City, Saskatchewan, who have perished on a faulty roller coaster called The Cyclone. Each tells their story, in song form, to win the reward from a fortune teller machine: the chance to return to life.

## Features
* **Explore the Town:** Navigate from your bedroom, through the house, out to Nuclear Ave, and into the local shops and mall because this is a sandbox
* **Character Interactions & Affection System:** Build relationships with a dope cast including Constance Blackwood, Noel Gruber, Mischa Bachinski, Ricky Potts, Jane Doe, and Ocean O'Connell Rosenberg.
* **Study & Skills:** Read books from your bookshelf to level up your stats (Literature, History, Physics, Music).
* **Inventory & Economy:** Earn money, buy snacks, clothes, gifts adn ecetera at the Mega Shopping Mall!
